Closed Testing Setup Guide

Launch Your App with Confidence

Follow these 4 simple steps in your Google Play Console to prepare your app for 14-day closed testing with the Testers Plus community.

Step 1

Select All Countries

In Google Play Console, open Countries/Regions, select All countries (recommended), and save the change.

This keeps the closed test available for Testers Plus testers in different regions.

Step 2

Add the Testers Plus Google Group

Go to Play Console → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing. Open Testers, choose Google Groups, and add:

testerplus@googlegroups.com

Complete your app bundle, store listing, and content rating before sending the closed test for review.

Step 3

Submit Changes for Review

Review the pending changes and click Send changes for review. After Google approves the closed testing release, the tester opt-in link can be shared.

Approval time can vary. Keep all release details accurate to avoid delays.

Step 4

Copy the Tester Opt-in URL

Once the closed test is approved, copy the tester opt-in URL from Play Console and submit that URL in Testers Plus. This is the link testers will use to join your app test.

  • Copy the URL exactly as shown in Play Console.
  • Paste it in the Testers Plus submission screen.
  • Wait for testers to start joining the closed test.

Ready to start testing?

Ensure all 4 steps are completed properly before submitting to our community.
