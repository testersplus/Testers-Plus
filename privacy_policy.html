Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Testers Plus, we prioritize your privacy. We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with our services. This includes:

  • Information provided during account registration (e.g., email addresses).
  • Details about the applications you submit for closed testing.
  • Device information and app usage statistics to help us improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to facilitate the closed testing process and improve our community experience. We use your data to:

  • Match apps with testers from our community.
  • Provide support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Enhance the security and functionality of the Testers Plus app.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your app details and testing URLs are only shared within our secure community of testers as part of the closed testing process you initiated.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and application details from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
